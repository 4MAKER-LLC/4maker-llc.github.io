The security of your data is important to us. 3. Disclosure to Advertisers & Location Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), account/email identifiers, and location signals with our advertising and platform partners (such as Google AdMob and Google Play Services) to support app functionality, serve geographically relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Approximate Location: Google AdMob automatically infers your general geographic area (such as city or country) using your device's IP address.
  • Precise Location (If Permitted): If you explicitly grant location permissions through your device's operating system, our app may share precise geographic location data (via GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular networks) with Google AdMob to deliver location-based advertisements.

To learn more about how Google collects and processes data from apps that use its advertising and platform services, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your Choices & Opt-Out: You can disable location sharing at any time through your device's system settings. You may also opt out of personalized advertising by resetting or deleting your device's Advertising ID in your device settings.
