3. Disclosure to Advertisers & Location Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and location signals with our advertising partner (Google AdMob) to serve geographically relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Approximate Location: Google AdMob automatically infers your general geographic area (such as city or country) using your device's IP address.

To learn more about how Google collects and processes data from apps that use its advertising services, please review Google’s Privacy & Terms at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your Choices & Opt-Out: You can disable location sharing at any time through your device's system settings. You may also opt out of personalized advertising by resetting or deleting your device's Advertising ID in your device settings.
