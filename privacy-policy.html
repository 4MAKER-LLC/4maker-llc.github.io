4MAKER LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected in connection with your use of our mobile application, Bounce Color Match.

1. Information Collection and Advertising

Bounce Color Match utilizes Google AdMob to serve advertisements within the application. AdMob and its third-party advertising partners may automatically collect certain device-level information to deliver, measure, and personalize ads:

  • Advertising Identifier: Android Advertising ID (GAID) used for frequency capping and ad personalization.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and language settings.
  • IP Address: Used to estimate approximate geographic location for localized ad targeting.
  • Diagnostic & Usage Data: Information regarding how ads are rendered, clicked, or viewed within the app.

For further details on how Google processes user data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms.

2. Children's Privacy & Google Play Families Policy

Bounce Color Match is designed to comply with Google Play Developer Policies regarding COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and regional child safety standards. If our application is directed at children or accessible to younger audiences, we enforce non-personalized ad settings and restrict advertising networks from collecting persistent identifiers for behavioral targeting.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information collected automatically by Google AdMob is shared strictly for advertising delivery, analytics, fraud prevention, and performance tracking.

4. User Opt-Out & Privacy Rights

You can manage or disable personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads).
  • Enable "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" depending on your Android OS version.
